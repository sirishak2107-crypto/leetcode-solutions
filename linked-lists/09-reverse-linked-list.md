## Problem: Reverse Linked List (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-linked-list/

### Approach

I used three pointers: previous, current, and next. I changed the direction of each link one by one until the entire linked list was reversed.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

The original linked list is reversed by changing the next pointers of each node.
